Concept & Vision

Her Space is a personal portfolio and digital sanctuary designed to express identity, creativity, and thoughtful interaction.

  • Feminine, soft, and minimal visual atmosphere.
  • Calming, clutter-free user experience.
  • Gentle pastel tones replacing stark high-contrast darks.
  • Warm, welcoming space that feels uniquely personal.
✨

Purpose & Objective

Built as part of AI 413 Interactive Systems Design, fulfilling all core and advanced assignment criteria:

  • Multi-screen navigation architecture with persistent state.
  • Personalized profile data for Nourhan Qamar.
  • Harmonious typography and Material 3 design tokens.
  • Production-ready Android and Web cross-platform support.
📱

Core Sections

The application is thoughtfully divided into three cohesive, dedicated destinations:

  • Home: Welcoming greeting, hero illustration, and quick call to action.
  • About Me: Portrait, biography, inspiring quote, and 8 interest chips.
  • Contact: Direct communication cards and social placeholder badges.

Architecture & Flow

Technical Implementation Flow

Complete verified architecture pipeline from entry point through navigation to presentation & assets
06 / 07
🚀 Application Entry Point lib/main.dart

main() initializes Flutter & runs HerSpaceApp. Root MaterialApp configures theme and launches MainNavigation.

↓
🌸 Design System app_theme.dart

Material 3 light theme from seed #E8A7B8, permanent AppBar theme & warm plum ink text.

🗂️ Single Source of Truth profile_data.dart

Centralized constants for Qamar: full name, bio, quote, contact info, and 8 curated interests.

↓
🧭 Main Navigation Controller main_navigation.dart

Stateful wrapper managing IndexedStack for zero-reload tab switching + Material 3 NavigationBar with active indicators.

↓
🏠 Home home_page.dart

Greeting, hero image, tagline card & CTA to About.

👤 About about_page.dart

Avatar fallback, bio, quote & 8 interest chips.

💌 Contact contact_page.dart

Email, phone, location & social placeholders.

↓
✨ Reusable Component soft_card.dart

Modular card with 24px rounded corners, white fill, and subtle rose elevation shadow.

📦 Assets & Native Branding pubspec.yaml

Bundled illustrations, adaptive launcher icons, and warm cream native splash drawables.

🎨 Material 3 Theming

Strictly light feminine pastel theme with seed color #E8A7B8 and custom AppBar title. Absolutely zero dark or black elements.

⚡ Stateful Navigation

IndexedStack retains all three screens in the widget tree, preserving scroll offsets and eliminating reload latency.

🧩 Clean Modular Decoupling

Clear separation between data (lib/data/), theme (lib/theme/), screens (lib/screens/), and widgets (lib/widgets/).

✅ Verified Cross-Platform

Tested on Android 17 (Samsung Galaxy S25) and Web PWA. Clean static analysis with 0 lint warnings and 100% test pass rate.

Concept & Vision

Her Space is a personal portfolio and digital sanctuary designed to express identity, creativity, and thoughtful interaction.

  • Feminine, soft, and minimal visual atmosphere.
  • Calming, clutter-free user experience.
  • Gentle pastel tones replacing stark high-contrast darks.
  • Warm, welcoming space that feels uniquely personal.
✨

Purpose & Objective

Built as part of AI 413 Interactive Systems Design, fulfilling all core and advanced assignment criteria:

  • Multi-screen navigation architecture with persistent state.
  • Personalized profile data for Nourhan Qamar.
  • Harmonious typography and Material 3 design tokens.
  • Production-ready Android and Web cross-platform support.
📱

Core Sections

The application is thoughtfully divided into three cohesive, dedicated destinations:

  • Home: Welcoming greeting, hero illustration, and quick call to action.
  • About Me: Portrait, biography, inspiring quote, and 8 interest chips.
  • Contact: Direct communication cards and social placeholder badges.

🎨 UI & Component Layer

🧭 State-Preserving Navigation
Uses IndexedStack wrapped in Material 3 Scaffold for instantaneous zero-latency tab switching while maintaining scroll offsets across Home, About, and Contact.
✨ Reusable SoftCard Widget
Standardized modular container with 20px rounded corners, subtle rose borders, and soft glowing elevation across all pages.
